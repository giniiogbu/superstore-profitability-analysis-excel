# Superstore Profitability Analysis

## Project Summary
An Excel-based analysis of the Sample Superstore dataset to identify which 
regions, states, and product categories are driving profit — and which are 
hurting it.

---

## Business Problem
The business needed to understand why overall profit margins were inconsistent 
across regions and product categories. Specifically: where are we losing money, 
and what is causing it?

---

## Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle Sample Superstore Dataset
- **Contents:** Orders across regions, states, product categories, and subcategories

---

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
  - Pivot Tables
  - Charts & Dashboards
  - Formulas

---

## Key Findings
- The **West region** is the most profitable, with **California** leading all states
- The **Central region** is the least profitable, with **Texas** recording significant losses
- The **Furniture category** in Texas is the biggest problem area
- Heavy discounting on furniture products is directly eroding profit margins

---

## Recommendations
- **Discontinue or reprice low-margin furniture products**, particularly in Texas
- **Review the discounting strategy** in the Central region — discounts are 
  not translating to profitable sales
- **Replicate the West region's approach** in underperforming regions to 
  improve overall margins
